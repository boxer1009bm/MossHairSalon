Our Policies

At Moss Salon, every appointment is reserved specifically for you. We value your time and appreciate you valuing ours as well.

Appointment Changes
We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Late Cancellations
Appointment canceled with less than 24 hours' notice will be charged 50% of the scheduled service total.

No-Shows
Clients who do not show up for their appointment without contacting us will be charged 100% of the scheduled service total.

Late Arrivals
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or reschedule your appointment to avoid running behind for other guests. A cancellation fee may apply if we are unable to complete your scheduled service.

Card on File
A volid credit card is required to reserve appointments. Your card will only be charged in accordance with our cancellation and no-show policy.

Emergencies
We understand that unexpected emergencies happen. If you experience an emergency, please reach out to us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate the situation.

Thank you for respecting our time and allowing us to provide the best experience for every guest. We can't wait to welcome you to Moss Salon.
